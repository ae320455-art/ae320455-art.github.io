Client Trust & Confidentiality

A short, plain-language statement of how I handle client data, systems, and findings — so you know exactly what to expect before we start working together.

01 confidentiality.log

Anything you share with me — logs, credentials, network details, incident data, business context — is treated as strictly confidential. It is used only for the scope of work we've agreed on, never disclosed to a third party, and never referenced publicly (including in my own case studies) without your explicit written permission.

02 handling_policy.log
  • Data is only accessed on secured, personal devices — never shared environments
  • Findings and reports are delivered directly to you, not stored longer than necessary
  • Access credentials you provide are used solely for the agreed task and discarded afterward
  • I flag anything sensitive I encounter beyond the original scope instead of acting on it unilaterally
03 nda.log

Happy to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before any engagement begins — whether it's yours or one I can provide. If your organization has a standard confidentiality process, I'll follow it exactly.
